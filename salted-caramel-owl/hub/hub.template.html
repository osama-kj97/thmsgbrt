Salted Caramel Owl · Pre-production · 23 Nov 2026

Hollowguard

A portrait, one-thumb tower defense. You hold back the night one lantern at a time: you can only build on lit ground, shadow creatures snuff your lights, and only your owls keep fighting in the dark.

The hook, on one card
  1. R1Light lets you build. Every tile is lit or dark; you can build and upgrade only on lit spots.
  2. R2Lightbound needs light. Squirrel, mushroom and crystal towers hit hardest, but sleep in the dark.
  3. R3Owls don't. Owl towers are weaker per sap, but they fight on in darkness.
  4. R4The night takes ground back. It creeps down from the top, and Saboteurs snuff your lanterns.

    What's in the first world

    Six towers in two classes, one hero, eight enemies and a boss across 10 levels of World 1, "The Rootwood". Every tier-3 tower splits into two branches. Neither branch strictly beats the other, so there is no single "super tower".

    Difficulty: one idea per level

    Bar height shows relative difficulty. Levels that introduce a new tool ease off; levels that mix tools spike.

    Hero

    Umber the Lamplighter, an eagle-owl. His lantern-staff is a light that moves with him, so wherever he parks becomes buildable. Dawnburst lights a wide area for 8 s; Silent Dive stuns and picks off Saboteurs.
